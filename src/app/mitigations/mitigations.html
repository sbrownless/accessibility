Accessibility patterns

400% Zoom Mitigations

Four focused examples for keeping grid content visible, operable, and readable at high zoom.

01

Text wrapping

Long cell content wraps onto additional lines instead of disappearing behind an ellipsis.

02

Keyboard auto-scroll

Use Tab or the arrow keys. The focused cell is brought into view automatically.

03

Strong focus

The active cell has a high-contrast outline that remains visible during keyboard navigation.

04

Detail panel

Select a row to reveal its complete notes in a persistent, keyboard-reachable panel.
